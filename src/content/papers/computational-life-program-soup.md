## Why it is here

This is the anchor paper for the program-soup ALife project. It gives a small substrate where replication, interaction, and selection can be studied without hiding everything behind a large simulator.

## Reading angle

- Translate the substrate into executable tests.
- Separate paper-faithful reproduction from useful ablations.
- Track which metrics actually identify replication rather than activity.
