## Why it is here

This is the conceptual bridge between maximum-likelihood style RL, control, graphical models, and entropy-regularized decision making.

## Reading angle

- Read it as a unifying lens, not only a recipe for an algorithm.
- Track how rewards become likelihood terms and policies become posterior inference.
- Pair with SAC, VIME, and active inference.
