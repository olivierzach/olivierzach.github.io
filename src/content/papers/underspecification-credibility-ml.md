## Why it is here

This is a core paper for thinking about why one metric and one train/test split are not enough.

## Reading angle

- Look for degrees of freedom left unresolved by the training objective.
- Evaluate robustness across plausible deployments, not just held-out rows.
- Treat repeatability as an engineering requirement.
