## Why it is here

TabPFN is one of the cleanest entry points for prior-data fitted networks and fast small-data classification.

## Reading angle

- Treat the prior as a modeling assumption, not magic.
- Test where the synthetic pretraining distribution matches real tabular data.
- Compare against simple baselines before adding complexity.
