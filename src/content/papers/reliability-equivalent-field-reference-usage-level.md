## Why it is here

This is Frank Sun's reliability-equivalence paper for choosing a field reference usage level when both customer usage and usage-to-failure are random.

## Reading angle

- Read it as an engineering bridge between distributed field usage and life-test design.
- Focus on the stress-strength framing and the reliability-equivalent usage percentile.
- Useful for avoiding arbitrary 90th/95th/99th percentile test assumptions when the fleet-level target is what matters.
