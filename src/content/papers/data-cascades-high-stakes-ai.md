## Why it is here

The paper names a failure mode that shows up constantly in applied ML: upstream data problems become downstream model behavior.

## Reading angle

- Ask where data assumptions enter the system.
- Track how small collection errors propagate.
- Make data quality visible before model work absorbs the blame.
