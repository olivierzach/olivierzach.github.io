## Why it is here

PaDiM is a clean industrial anomaly baseline: pretrained patch features plus local distribution modeling.

## Reading angle

- Read it as a strong classical-statistics layer on top of deep features.
- Compare patch Gaussian assumptions against memory-bank retrieval.
- Keep localization quality separate from image-level classification.
