## Why it is here

This paper is useful because it puts e-values in the broader argument about what should count as statistical evidence.

## Reading angle

- Compare e-values against Bayes factors and likelihood ratios without collapsing them into the same object.
- Track where interpretability and optional stopping guarantees diverge.
- Useful for building a personal statistical toolkit around evidence, not just significance.
