## Why it is here

Useful as a checklist paper for keeping experiments from drifting into unearned claims.

## Reading angle

- Watch for leakage, weak baselines, and ambiguous problem framing.
- Prefer claims that survive replication.
- Keep development process in scope, not just final numbers.
