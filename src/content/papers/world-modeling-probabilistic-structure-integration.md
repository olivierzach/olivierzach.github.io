## Why it is here

This is part of the newer probabilistic deep learning batch. It is interesting because it frames world modeling as controllable probabilistic structure integration rather than only next-state prediction.

## Reading angle

- Identify what structural variables the model makes promptable.
- Compare against latent dynamics models and diffusion-style world models.
- Look for ways to evaluate whether structure is genuinely reusable.
