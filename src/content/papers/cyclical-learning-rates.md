## Why it is here

Learning-rate schedules are a practical lever, and this paper is a good reminder that simple training dynamics can matter more than model novelty.

## Reading angle

- Use schedules as experimental controls.
- Track sensitivity, not just the best run.
- Treat optimization behavior as observable, not mystical.
