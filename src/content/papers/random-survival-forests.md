## Why it is here

Random survival forests are a strong applied baseline for nonlinear time-to-event work.

## Reading angle

- Use this as the non-neural baseline for survival projects.
- Pay attention to how censoring changes splitting and aggregation.
- Compare variable importance against neural representations.
