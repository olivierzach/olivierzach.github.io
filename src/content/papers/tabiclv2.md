Tabular foundation models are moving quickly, and this paper is useful for understanding the newer open TabICL direction.

## Reading angle

- Compare against strong classical baselines, not weak defaults.
- Track inference cost alongside accuracy.
- Look for settings where in-context prediction is operationally worth it.
