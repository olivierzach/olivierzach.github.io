## Why it is here

This is a bridge between modern prediction models and valid inference. It belongs in the corpus because it asks how learned predictions can help when labeled data are scarce.

## Reading angle

- Watch how prediction error is controlled rather than ignored.
- Compare against prediction-powered inference based on confidence intervals.
- Useful for thinking about ML-assisted science and medical evidence.
