## Why it is here

This paper sharpens the time-dependence question for machine consciousness: what matters is not only what is computed, but when and how the computation is realized.

## Reading angle

- Look for criteria that distinguish sequential simulation from a unified conscious moment.
- Connect the argument to language agents with fragmented state and tool-mediated memory.
- Use it as a philosophical stress test for claims about persistent AI selves.
