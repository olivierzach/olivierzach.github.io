## Why it is here

This is the transformer reference point. It is still worth reading directly because so much later tooling assumes its vocabulary.

## Reading angle

- Track what attention replaces and what it does not.
- Keep positional information, scaling, and masking details explicit.
- Read it as systems design as much as model design.
