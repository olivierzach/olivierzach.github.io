## Why it is here

WinCLIP is a good contrast case for the anomaly shelf: anomaly detection with a vision-language prior instead of only task-specific normal-image training.

## Reading angle

- Compare zero-shot and few-normal-shot setups against classic MVTec-style training.
- Watch the prompt and window aggregation choices.
- Useful for thinking about industrial anomaly work when labeled data is thin.
