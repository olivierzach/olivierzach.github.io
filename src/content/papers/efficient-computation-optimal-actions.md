## Why it is here

Todorov's linearly solvable control work is a useful older anchor for the idea that some control problems become tractable after the right probabilistic transformation.

## Reading angle

- Look for the structural assumptions that make optimal actions efficient.
- Compare with control-as-inference and maximum-entropy RL.
- Useful for understanding where elegant theory does and does not transfer to messy RL settings.
