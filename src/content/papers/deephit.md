## Why it is here

DeepHit is a core deep survival baseline for competing risks and discrete event-time modeling.

## Reading angle

- Separate the likelihood term from the ranking term.
- Check how censoring is represented in the target distribution.
- Use it as a benchmark for newer dynamic and mixture-based survival models.
