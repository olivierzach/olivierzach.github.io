## Why it is here

This is the Deep SVDD paper: a direct deep one-class objective for anomaly detection rather than a feature extractor followed by a separate one-class method.

## Reading angle

- Compare the hypersphere objective against one-class neural networks and classical SVDD.
- Watch the architectural constraints that prevent collapse.
- Use it as a core baseline for anomaly detection methods trained on normal-only data.
