## Why it is here

DSM is a useful bridge from classical survival analysis into representation learning for censored outcomes and competing risks.

## Reading angle

- Treat the parametric mixture choice as a modeling commitment.
- Compare the objective against Cox-style partial likelihood and discrete-time hazard models.
- Look for calibration and subgroup behavior, not just concordance.
