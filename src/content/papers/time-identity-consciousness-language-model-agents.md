## Why it is here

This is the most directly relevant Bennett paper for evaluating language-model agents, because it asks whether language and tool use preserve the identity conditions needed for serious consciousness claims.

## Reading angle

- Separate convincing self-report from persistent identity.
- Track what memory, timing, and agency assumptions the paper requires.
- Pair with implementation work on agent state, context windows, and episodic memory.
