## Why it is here

This is another recent Zotero annotation cluster. It belongs next to probabilistic deep learning and reliability work because it tries to give language-model uncertainty a probabilistic interpretation rather than a heuristic score.

## Reading angle

- Separate semantic uncertainty from raw token uncertainty.
- Ask whether the proposed uncertainty measure is calibrated enough for high-stakes decisions.
- Compare against Bayesian, ensemble, conformal, and abstention baselines.

## Annotation trail

The annotations focus on the paper's random-walk framing of input-output uncertainty and the attempt to turn semantic similarity into a usable probabilistic object.
