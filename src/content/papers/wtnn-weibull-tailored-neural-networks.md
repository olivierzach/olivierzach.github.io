## Why it is here

This is a modern Weibull-tailored survival model that belongs with the reliability and time-to-event work.

## Reading angle

- Track how the Weibull assumption is encoded in the network and loss.
- Compare against nonparametric survival baselines before treating neural survival as automatically better.
- Watch calibration, not only ranking metrics.
