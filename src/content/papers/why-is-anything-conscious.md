## Why it is here

This is part of the recent Bennett consciousness batch in Zotero. It is a useful counterweight to purely behavioral evaluations of AI systems.

## Reading angle

- Start from embodied, self-organizing organisms rather than disembodied text behavior.
- Treat consciousness claims as constraints on organization, identity, and interpretation.
- Compare the paper's biological commitments against language-agent accounts.
