## Why it is here

MVTec AD is the dataset backbone behind much of the modern industrial anomaly literature.

## Reading angle

- Use this to understand the benchmark assumptions before comparing methods.
- Separate defect detection from defect localization.
- Watch category-specific variance; one score can hide very different failure modes.
