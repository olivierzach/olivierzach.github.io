## Why it is here

This is the attention-residuals paper from the latest Zotero pass. It makes residual structure explicit inside the attention score path, which pairs well with the ResNet and Transformer papers already on the shelf.

## Reading angle

- Separate residual pathways over activations from residual pathways over attention logits.
- Read it beside identity mappings in ResNets and vanilla Transformer attention.
- Ask whether the gain is architectural bias, optimization smoothing, or both.
