## Why it is here

This gives the anomaly shelf a one-class neural baseline, useful when labeled defects are scarce.

## Reading angle

- Compare learned one-class objectives against classical one-class SVM intuition.
- Separate representation learning from boundary fitting.
- Useful as a baseline before moving to patch-memory or multimodal systems.
