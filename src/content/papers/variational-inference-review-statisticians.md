## Why it is here

This is a statistical foundation piece for the probabilistic modeling shelf. It helps connect Bayesian approximation, ELBO objectives, VAEs, active inference, and RL-as-inference.

## Reading angle

- Keep the optimization/inference tradeoff explicit.
- Track what the variational family rules out.
- Use it as a reference when newer ML papers invoke ELBOs too casually.
