## Why it is here

Neural operators are a useful scientific-ML substrate, and this paper adds a generative uncertainty layer to function-to-function prediction.

## Reading angle

- Separate discretization-invariant prediction from calibrated stochastic outputs.
- Ask whether the diffusion last layer captures epistemic or aleatoric uncertainty.
- Useful for scientific systems where one deterministic field prediction is not enough.
