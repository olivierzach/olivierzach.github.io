## Why it is here

This is a useful production ML paper because it connects model structure to memorization, generalization, and serving constraints.

## Reading angle

- Watch how feature engineering and model architecture share responsibility.
- Ask what the serving system must support.
- Treat recommendations as an end-to-end product loop.
